Our Heritage & Craft

Born in the cultural heart of Pakistan, Luxe Bags bridges centuries of leather craftsmanship with contemporary haute couture aesthetics.

Crafted on MM Alam Road

Luxe Bags was established with a singular vision: to craft luxury handbags that rival international legacy houses right here in Lahore, Pakistan. Our flagship atelier located in Gulberg III serves as both a design studio and boutique.

Each bag undergoes over 36 hours of hand-cutting, edge-painting, stitching, and metal assembly by seasoned Pakistani leather artisans.

Sustainably Sourced Leather

We take immense pride in our materials. From supple blush nappa to rich mocha grain and structured caramel finishes, all leather is ethically sourced and vegetable-tanned to ensure durability and eco-conscious elegance.
